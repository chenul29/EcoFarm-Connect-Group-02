═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  HARVEST PREDICTION CALCULATION EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Simple Date Math:
  1. Get today's date (Nov 24, 2025)
  2. Get crop's harvest date (e.g., Feb 24, 2026)
  3. Calculate difference in days: 92 days
  4. Show status based on days:
     - Negative days = ⚠️ Overdue
     - 0 days = ✅ Today!
     - 1-7 days = 🔔 This Week
     - 8-30 days = 📅 This Month
     - 30+ days = ⏰ Future

Example:
  Today: Nov 24, 2025
  Harvest: Feb 24, 2026
  Calculation: Feb 24 - Nov 24 = 92 days
  Status: ⏰ Future (more than 30 days)
